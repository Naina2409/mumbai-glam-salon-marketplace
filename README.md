**Mumbai's AI-Powered Beauty Salon Marketplace**

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Mumbaikars struggle to find reliable salons that understand their specific needs—especially during monsoon season when humidity causes frizzy hair.

## 💡 Our Solution

**Mumbai Glam** connects users with the perfect salon in under 60 seconds using:
- 🤖 AI Stylist Chatbot for personalized recommendations
- 📍 Curated salons across Bandra, Andheri, Juhu, Powai, and more
- 🌧️ Specialized "Monsoon Hair Care" packages

## 🤖 AI Workflow Explanation

1. **AI Stylist Chatbot**: Uses rule-based natural language processing to understand user queries about:
   - Hair concerns (frizzy, dry, hair fall)
   - Occasions (wedding, casual, party)
   - Budget preferences (budget, luxury)
   - Specific services (haircut, spa, coloring)

2. **Response Logic**: Matches keywords to recommend relevant salons with specific pricing and locations.

3. **Future Enhancement**: Will integrate actual ChatGPT API for more dynamic conversations.
